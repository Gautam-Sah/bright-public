Enrollment Open

Join the BPS Educational Family

Bright Public School welcomes students of all backgrounds who demonstrate a strong desire to learn, grow, and contribute positively to our student community. Admissions are open annually for Play Group, Kindergarten, Primary, and Higher Secondary (+2 Science & Management) programs.

We advise parents to initiate registration early to reserve seats, schedule placement evaluations, and secure document clearance ahead of the school term start.

Procedure

Step-by-Step Admission Process

A straightforward enrollment path designed to support parent convenience and verify student placement.

1

Submit Admission Inquiry

Complete the online Inquiry Form below or visit our school administration office near the C.D.O. Office in Ghantaghar-9, Birgunj to collect a prospectus.

2

Complete Application Form

Fill out the detailed admission form with accurate personal, parent contact, and academic history details.

3

Submit Required Documents

Provide physical photocopies of birth certificates, previous transcripts/report cards, and passport-sized photographs to the registrar.

4

Student Evaluation & Interview

A brief placement test and friendly interview are conducted to understand student interests, concept clarity, and class readiness.

5

Complete Enrollment & Fee Clearances

Once admission is approved, complete the admission fee deposits, collect text books, uniform guidelines, and academic calendars.

Checklist

Required Documents for Registration

Please prepare clean, legible photocopies of the following records to attach to the official application paperwork:

  • Student Birth Certificate: Issued by municipal or ward offices.
  • Recent Photos: Four copies of passport-sized student color photos.
  • Academic History: Marksheets/grade reports from the previous school (for Class I and above).
  • Transfer Certificate: Original transfer/character certificate from the last school attended.
  • Guardian Identification: Citizenship certificate copy of the father, mother, or legal guardian.
